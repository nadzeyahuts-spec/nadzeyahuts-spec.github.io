Background

EarthByt is a white-label carbon-credit marketplace for enterprise climate action. Buyers purchase and retire verified on-chain credits, issuers onboard and list projects, and operators run compliance from an admin portal.

The challenge

Three roles, regulated onboarding (KYB, UBO, AML), a marketplace, checkout, retirement certificates and ESG reporting — and the client needed to see all of it working, in two themes, before committing to development.

Approach

I turned the client’s low-fidelity wireframes into a structured prompt per role and flow, generated every screen in Claude Design and connected them into one navigable product. A shared token system drives the dark and light themes; I hand-tuned hierarchy, tables and status colours so compliance states read at a glance.
