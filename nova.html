Background

The CTO needed two interactive prototypes for prospective iGaming clients: a rocket-style crash game and 30-second crypto price-prediction rounds. Not slides — products the client can actually play.

The challenge

In one short demo, answer the three questions every buyer asks: do they understand my product, can they do blockchain — not just buttons — and how much will it cost?

Approach

I studied the genre leaders (Aviator, Bustabit, PancakeSwap Prediction, Polymarket), wrote a full scenario and storyboard — three acts, deterministic rounds, exact timings — and built both games with Claude. Each demo deliberately shows a loss, proves fairness with a working verify modal, and ends on a scope-and-stack CTA at the emotional peak.

Product Features

One design language for both games: deep space, violet primary, neon accents for wins and losses, and a tech panel for the people who check the maths.

Demo 1

NOVA Crash

Rising multiplier

The only mechanic that needs no explanation: a rocket, a growing multiplier and a Cash Out button with a live payout.

A live bets feed of 40 players adds social pressure.

Auto cash-out & boosts

Auto cash-out keeps casual players in the game; boosts and shields act as retention tools, not decoration.

NOVA Pulse
