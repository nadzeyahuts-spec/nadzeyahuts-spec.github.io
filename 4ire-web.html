Background

4IRE builds compliance-grade software for banks, fintechs and regulated enterprises: digital banking, tokenized assets, stablecoins and AI-driven finance. The site had to speak to institutions — and look like a company that ships.

The client’s brief

A light, glass theme: airy and premium, but still institutional and trustworthy, with clear paths to “Schedule a consultation”.

Approach

I designed the key pages and the glass visual language by hand, then used Figma AI tools for the repetitive work: the glass icon set, micro-animations, responsive variants and recurring blocks — case cards, logo strips, FAQs. That freed my time for hierarchy, layout and an SEO-friendly page structure.
