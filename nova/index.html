How to run the demo
1 Auto mode

Both prototypes play themselves in a scripted, deterministic loop. Same outcome every run — safe to rehearse against.

2 Take over

Click any control to seize manual play mid-round. Space cashes out in Crash; ↑ ↓ place bets in Pulse.

3 Guided tour

Press G for a six-stop walkthrough framed around business outcomes, or T to open the technical panel.
