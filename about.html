About.

Senior Product Designer · UX/UIAI Product Builder

I’m Nadzeya — a product designer with 16 years in design and 6 in UX/UI for fintech, banking and Web3. I make complex financial products feel simple and trustworthy: neobanks, RWA tokenization and DAO platforms, crypto games, carbon marketplaces and low-code tools.

I work end to end — research, flows, UI and design systems — and I build too. With Claude, Figma AI and a team of AI agents I turn ideas into working prototypes, websites and apps in hours, then grow them with analytics and SEO.

I work remotely with product teams, founders and engineers across the US, Europe and beyond.
