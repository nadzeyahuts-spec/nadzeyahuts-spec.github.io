Background

4IRE Group — a blockchain product studio — was tokenizing physical assets: copper, gold, real estate, data centres, and green energy bonds. The challenge was not technical. It was human. The platform had to speak DeFi to one audience and traditional finance to another — at the same time, on the same screen.

My scope — user-facing product only

Full design of the investor-facing platform: MetaMask wallet connect, marketplace with asset grid and category filters, overview, my portfolio, asset detail pages, and Buy / Sell flows with Crypto / Fiat payment and KYC at the point of intent. Delivered in dark and light themes, for desktop and mobile.

Work in progress – full study is coming soon…

Product Features

A calm, institutional design language for six real-world asset classes — dark and light themes, clear status badges (Live / Soon / TBA) and live price data. The design system is built on Figma Variables for color, spacing and component variants, so both themes share one source of truth.

Asset Detail — Overview Tab

Sticky right-rail with price, projected growth, available tokens, and Purchase CTA. Purple KYC banner surfaces verification at point of intent, not at registration.

Buy RWA Modal

Two-panel layout: asset preview left, payment details right. Crypto/Fiat toggle, quantity shortcuts (10%/25%/50%/Max), live USDT calculation, and a help link for crypto newcomers.

Sell Tokens Modal

Two-panel layout: asset preview left, payment details right. “I want to sell” / “I want to receive” radio button, quantity shortcuts (10%/25%/50%/Max), live USDT calculation, and a help link for crypto newcomers.
