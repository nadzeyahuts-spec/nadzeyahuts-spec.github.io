Background

NeoBankX is an enterprise-grade crypto-fiat bridge for seamless, secure and compliant financial operations. It brings crypto and fiat under one account: multicurrency balances, cards, swaps, transfers and compliance monitoring — across a mobile app, a web portal and an admin portal.

The challenge

Bridge two worlds that usually feel separate — the speed and openness of crypto and the trust and compliance rails of banking — inside a single, coherent product. The system had to stay modular: every institution should be able to switch modules on or off to match its own financial needs.

Approach

A component-driven design system built on Material Design 3 and Figma Variables, so the mobile, web and admin surfaces share one language. Flows were designed around real financial logic — KYC/AML, SWIFT/SEPA, multicurrency accounting and on-chain operations — not just screens.

One account · two worlds

Crypto and fiat, unified.

NeoBankX handles both native crypto operations and full fiat banking in the same interface — with compliance and multicurrency accounting built in from the start.

Crypto

  • Crypto operations — deposit, withdraw, purchase.
  • CEX operations — swaps, cross-currency swaps and token listing.
  • Analytics view — balance dashboard, 24h change and candlestick charts per token.

Fiat

  • Transaction management & monitoring — KYC and AML.
  • Fiat operations — deposit, withdraw, exchange and crypto purchase.
  • Transfers — P2P, account-to-account, card-to-card and international SWIFT / SEPA.
  • Multicurrency accounts — set-up and management.

Product Features

A dark, focused interface with a violet primary for actions and values. Built as a component system so screens stay consistent from the admin web portal down to the mobile app.

Admin portal

Compliance & monitoring

Transaction monitoring · KYC / AML

Analytics dashboards and monitoring tools for the operations team — flag, review and resolve transactions with a full audit trail.

User verification states (including PEP / HIO status) are surfaced directly in the review flow.

Add customizable modules for your specific financial needs.

The platform is modular by design. For instance, you can add Apple Pay and Google Pay to process fiat, crypto and stablecoins — or switch whole capabilities on and off per institution.
